## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🦯 [AI Smart Stick](https://github.com/riyajclgn123/AI-Smart-Stick) · [Live dashboard](https://aismartstick.vercel.app/)
An assistive walking stick for visually impaired users. It detects **80+ object classes** with YOLOv8 and announces them through Bluetooth earbuds, adds ultrasonic distance sensing and haptic alerts, and streams to a caregiver dashboard with live alerts and GPS. Built on **under $200 of hardware**, with four 3D-printed prototypes.
<br/>`Raspberry Pi` `YOLOv8` `Python` `Flask` `SQLite` `Node.js` `Express` `SSE` `Leaflet` `Fusion 360`

### 🚗 [Car Recommender: AI Car Price Prediction](https://github.com/riyajclgn123/Car-Recommender)
A full-stack ML app that predicts fair US used-car prices and recommends cars by budget, income and lifestyle. It covers the full ML lifecycle on **3M listings (9.3 GB)**: cleaning, leakage audit, models from a NumPy gradient-descent baseline up to tuned LightGBM, SHAP explainability and deployment.
**Results: R² = 0.974 · MAE $1,633 · MAPE 6.3%**, tracked in MLflow.
<br/>`Python` `LightGBM` `Optuna` `SHAP` `MLflow` `FastAPI` `React` `Claude API`

### 🛒 [PriceHawk AI](https://github.com/riyajclgn123/PriceHawk_AI) · [Live demo](https://pricehawkai.vercel.app/)
A price-intelligence platform that scrapes Amazon, Flipkart, Shein and eBay, **forecasts 7-day price trends** with a gradient-boosting time-series model, and sends target-price email alerts from Redis-queued background workers.
<br/>`Next.js` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `Redis` `Playwright` `scikit-learn`

### 👁️ [Brixel](https://github.com/riyajclgn123/Brixel) · *LionHacks 2026*
A classroom assistant for visually impaired students, **built in six hours**. A Jetson Nano photographs the whiteboard and extracts the text with OCR. A live dashboard then reads each capture aloud and exports it as Braille (.brf), plain text or print.
<br/>`Jetson Nano` `Python` `OpenCV` `Google Cloud Vision` `Supabase Realtime` `React` `Web Speech API`

### 📊 [Louisiana EduPulse](https://github.com/riyajclgn123/Nexus_DevDays) · *Nexus Louisiana Dev Days 2025*
A role-based decision dashboard for policymakers, funders and school operators. It combines Louisiana K-12 performance, Census and BLS workforce data behind **12+ FastAPI endpoints** and includes a Llama 3.3 70B assistant that cites its sources.
<br/>`React` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `SQLAlchemy` `Groq` `scikit-learn`

### 🎮 [Cargo Games](https://github.com/riyajclgn123/Multiplayer_Game_Project_CMPS401)
A real-time multiplayer platform (UNO, Tic-Tac-Toe, Rock-Paper-Scissors) with room-code matchmaking and live chat. An async Rust server holds every game's rules and pushes state to all players over WebSockets. Built by a team of three.
<br/>`Rust` `Axum` `Tokio` `WebSockets` `React` `TypeScript`
